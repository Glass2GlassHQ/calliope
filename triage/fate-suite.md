## g2g bugs

Each bug names one reproducer. `S=$HOME/.cache/calliope` in every command. The tables below list every affected scenario with its own reproducer. "Expected" is what ffmpeg n8.1 and gstreamer 1.28 do with the same file. The 22 bugs of the 42c0a809 sweep are fixed and listed in TESTING.md "Bugs found".

1. **AAC in Matroska decodes to nothing and g2g-launch exits 0.** All three MKV AAC files in the corpus do this. Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/mkv/test7_cut.mkv ! decodebin ! audioconvert ! audio/x-raw,format=S16LE ! filesink location=/tmp/out.pcm` (AAC LC 48 kHz stereo, 2-byte CodecPrivate). Expected: the AAC track decodes. Got: AacParse and FfmpegAudioDec each take 48 buffers and emit nothing, a 0-byte file. Also `fate-suite/mkv/1242-small.mkv` and `gst-medias/defaults/matroska/test5.mkv`.
2. **Mp4Demux fails CapsMismatch on three AAC configurations.** Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/aac/al04sf_48.mp4 ! decodebin ! audioconvert ! fakesink` (AAC LC 48 kHz, channel configuration 0 with a PCE). Expected: mono AAC decodes. Got: `pipeline error in Mp4Demux0: CapsMismatch`, also on the retry without the stream. Same on `fate-suite/mpeg4/fragmented.mp4` (explicit SBR sync extension) and `gst-medias/defaults/exMPD_BIP_TC1/ElephantsDream_AAC48K_064.mp4` (implicit HE-AAC, a 24 kHz config in a 48 kHz sample entry). 91 other MP4 AAC files agree.
3. **AviDemux rejects a truncated AVI.** The file is refused when the RIFF size runs past the end of the file. Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/v210/v210_720p-partial.avi ! decodebin ! audioconvert ! fakesink` (RIFF size 30294016, file 3145728 bytes). Expected: the PCM up to the cut. Got: `pipeline error in AviDemux0: CapsMismatch`. All 10 rows are "-partial" samples or files cut at 1 MB.
4. **The MPEG-PS demuxer merges two MP2 streams into one.** Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/gst-medias/defaults/mpegps/sample_mpeg_program_stream_scr.mpg ! decodebin ! audioconvert ! audio/x-raw,format=S16LE,rate=48000,channels=2 ! filesink location=/tmp/out.pcm`. Expected: 294912 samples per channel from the first stream. Got: 589824, frames of both streams interleaved from sample 1153.
5. **TsDemux sends AC-3 in private PES to MpegAudioParse.** Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/ac3/mp3ac325-4864-small.ts ! decodebin ! audioconvert ! audio/x-raw,format=S16LE ! filesink location=/tmp/out.pcm` (AC-3 6 ch and 2 ch, stream_type 0x06). Expected: the 6-channel AC-3 decodes. Got: TsDemux ! MpegAudioParse ! FfmpegAudioDec, a 0-byte file and exit status 0.
6. **Demuxers do not map codecs g2g decodes.** MOV PCM (`sowt`, `twos`, `raw `, `alaw`, `ulaw`), MP3 in MP4 (`mp4a` and `.mp3`), Vorbis in MP4 (`msVo`), Matroska `A_PCM/INT/LIT`, mu-law in AVI and VP8 in Ogg. Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/qt-surge-suite/surge-2-16-L-sowt.mov ! decodebin ! audioconvert ! fakesink`. Expected: s16le PCM plays. Got: `decodebin: no decoder for sowt`. VP8 in Ogg (`fate-suite/ogg/videotest.ogv`) gets OpusDec for a video request.
7. **MjpegDec fails on AVI MJPEG frames without Huffman tables.** MJPEG in AVI may leave out DHT and rely on the default tables of ITU-T T.81 Annex K. Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/mjpeg/mjpeg_field_order.avi ! decodebin ! videoconvert ! video/x-raw,format=I420 ! fakesink`. Expected: 4:2:2 frames decode. Got: `pipeline error in MjpegDec0: CapsMismatch`. Also `gst-medias/defaults/avi/bowlerhatdancer.sleepytom.SGP.mjpeg.avi`.
8. **FLV H.264 loses frames.** Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/nellymoser/nellymoser-discont.flv ! decodebin ! videoconvert ! video/x-raw,format=I420 ! filesink location=/tmp/out.yuv`. Expected: 511 frames, ffmpeg and gstreamer agree. Got: 447, diverging at frame 86 where the timestamps jump. `fate-suite/flv/Enigma_Principles_of_Lust-part.flv` gives 295 frames for 350 after a corrupt NAL at frame 34 that ffmpeg decodes past.
9. **A second edit list entry is ignored.** Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/mov/mov-2elist-elist1-ends-bframe.mov ! decodebin ! video/x-raw,format=I420 ! filesink location=/tmp/out.yuv`. Expected: 42 frames, ffmpeg and gstreamer agree. Got: 48, the 6 frames at frame 18 are ones the first edit cuts.
10. **Chained Ogg FLAC stops at the first link.** Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/ogg-flac/chained-meta.ogg ! decodebin ! audioconvert ! audio/x-raw,format=S16LE ! filesink location=/tmp/out.pcm`. Expected: 17640 samples. Got: 4608, bit-exact with ffmpeg's first 4608. gstreamer times out.
11. **Unsupported codecs fail with a pipeline error instead of naming the missing decoder.** The diagnosis column marks these rows "(bug 11)". decodebin builds the video chain for an unsupported audio codec (DTS, LATM, pcm_dvd, Nellymoser, IMA ADPCM) and the audio chain for a video request on Theora or an attached picture. WavParse, StillImageParse and Mp4Demux fail CapsMismatch on codecs they do not map, FfmpegVideoDec fails NotConfigured on CAVS and VVC, and LATM in TS exits 0 with no output. Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/dts/dts.ts ! decodebin ! audioconvert ! fakesink`. Expected: `decodebin: no decoder for` DTS. Got: `AudioConvert produces no output for video/x-raw`. Low severity.
12. **FfmpegAudioDec fails Hardware(Other) at the end of a USAC stream.** Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/aac/usac/xhe_target_level.m4a ! decodebin ! audioconvert ! fakesink`. Expected: exit 0. Got: the same 192512 bytes ffmpeg writes, then `pipeline error in FfmpegAudioDec0: Hardware(Other)`.
13. **TsDemux loses an AC-3 frame across PMT version changes.** In `fate-suite/mpegts/pmtchange.ts` the PMT alternates versions 2 and 30. Reproducer: `g2g-launch -q filesrc location=$S/fate-suite/mpegts/pmtchange.ts ! decodebin ! audioconvert ! audio/x-raw,format=S16LE,rate=48000,channels=2 ! filesink location=/tmp/out.pcm`. Expected: 3072 samples per channel. Got: 1536.
